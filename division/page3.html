What is the "Old School" Method?

Long ago (before calculators! 🧓), people divided big numbers using the long division method — sometimes called the "bus stop" method 🚌. You write the numbers in a special bracket, then work digit by digit: divide, multiply, subtract, and bring down!

It looks like this: 20 ÷ 2 is written as 2 ) 20, and we solve it one step at a time. Watch the magic below! ✨
