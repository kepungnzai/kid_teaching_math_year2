🏷️ Angle Family Names

Just like people have first names, angles have family names based on how big their turn is!

🔶 Acute — smaller than 90° 🔷 Right — exactly 90° 🟠 Obtuse — between 90° and 180° ⚪ Straight — exactly 180° 🟢 Reflex — more than 180° 🔄 Full turn — exactly 360°

❓💡 Questions & Answers

Think about each question first, then press the button to reveal the answer. Explaining it to a friend makes you a real angle master! 🎉

1️⃣ What is an angle?

An angle is the amount of turn between two lines that meet at a point. The point where they meet is called the vertex. 📐

2️⃣ Why is 90° called a "right angle"?

Because it makes a perfect L shape — like the corners of a window, book, or square. It's the "right" (correct/straight) angle we use all the time! 📖

3️⃣ How is a 45° angle related to a 90° angle?

A 45° angle is half of a right angle! Fold a right-angle corner in half and you get exactly 45°. 🍕 That's why an 8-slice pizza has 45° slices — 360 ÷ 8 = 45.

4️⃣ What does a 180° angle look like?

A 180° angle is a perfectly straight line! Think of the clock hands at 6:00, or two rays pointing in exactly opposite directions. 🕕

5️⃣ What is a full turn in degrees?

A full turn is 360° — all the way around, like doing one complete twirl or going once around a circle. 🔄

6️⃣ What is 720°?

720° is two full turns — 360° + 360°! That's why skateboarders call their double-spin trick "the 720". 🛹

7️⃣ What are the names of the angle families?

From smallest to biggest: acute (less than 90°), right (exactly 90°), obtuse (90°–180°), straight (exactly 180°), reflex (more than 180°), and full turn (360°). 🏷️

8️⃣ Why are there 360° in a circle?

Long ago, people guessed there were about 360 days in a year, and 360 is also a super handy number with lots of divisors. So they chose 360 for a full turn — and we've used it ever since! 🌍
