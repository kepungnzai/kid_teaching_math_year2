Speed = Slope!

Your Module 1 superpower, driving a race car — and your first big calculus word!

🏎️ Watch a race car: after 1 hour it is 60 km away, after 2 hours 120 km, after 3 hours 180 km… Plot those dots on a graph and join them — you get a sloping line!

Its steepness (rise ÷ run) = distance ÷ time = SPEED 🚗💨. Speed is just a slope in disguise! And a speedometer showing how fast RIGHT NOW is called a derivative — your first real calculus word! 🎓

🎮 Demo 1: Race the Car!

Pick a speed, press GO, and watch the distance dots line up into a slope. Steeper line = faster car!

60 km/h
Slope = 0 km ÷ 0 h = ? km/h

Press GO to start the race! 🏁

📖 Read the graph: the car drove 120 km in 2 hours. Slope = 120 ÷ 2 = 60 km/h — exactly the speed! Try 100 km/h: after 2 hours the dot is at 200 km, and 200 ÷ 2 = 100. The slope of a distance graph IS the speed. 🚗💨

🛞 Demo 2: The Speedometer (derivative!)

A trip with traffic: fast highway, slow town, lunch stop 🍔. Drag the time slider — the speedometer shows the slope RIGHT NOW. That "right now" slope is the derivative!

⏱️ Speedometer: 0 km/h  

Flat line = parked (0 km/h). Steep line = zooming! The speedometer is a slope-detector. 🔍
