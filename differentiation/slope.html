How Steep Is It?

The secret seed of differentiation — measured with one tiny division!

⛷️ Meet Sasha the skier! On this page we learn to tell her story — the story of her motion down a mountain. First (Demo 1) we learn the one tool every story needs: measuring steepness = Drop ÷ Distance ⬇️➡️. Then (Demo 2) we follow Sasha down 3 chapters — steep (slope 2.0), gentle (0.5), super-steep (3.0) — and narrate what she feels, moment by moment.

Telling that motion story — "how fast is she going at THIS exact spot?" — is called differentiation, the first half of calculus! ❄️📖 Then two NEW stories with twists: a bouncing ball that STOPS mid-air ⚽🎈 and a rocket that keeps going FASTER 🚀!

🎮 Demo 1: How Steep Is One Hill?

Every story needs a measuring tool! Drag the sliders to build ONE hill and read its steepness: Drop ÷ Distance. Green = gentle 🐰, red = scary! 😱

Steepness = 3 ÷ 6 = 0.5

🐰 Gentle — like Sasha's middle chapter (0.5)!

📖 Your measuring tool: drop 3 m over 6 m → 3 ÷ 6 = 0.5 (gentle cruise 🐰). Drop 6 m over 3 m → 6 ÷ 3 = 2.0 (fast! 🟠). Drop 6 m over 2 m → 6 ÷ 2 = 3.0 (SCARY 😱). One tool measures every chapter of Sasha's story!

🏆 Chapter 3 (3.0) is 6× steeper than Chapter 2 (0.5) — that's why her story goes wheee… phew… AAAAARGH! Same 6 m of track, totally different feelings!

📖 Tell her story in 3 chapters: 📖 Chapter 1 (steep, 2.0): Sasha drops 12 m in 6 m — "Wheee, flying at 20 m/s!" 🚀 📖 Chapter 2 (gentle, 0.5): only 3 m down in 6 m — "Phew, cruising at 5 m/s, time for cocoa…" 🐰☕ 📖 Chapter 3 (super-steep, 3.0): 18 m down in 6 m — "AAAAH, 30 m/s, hold on!!" 😱 Differentiation = telling what each moment feels like. 🔍 But wait — HOW do you know what she feels at any spot? That's Demo 3's job! 👇

📖🎮 Demo 3: YOU Are the Storyteller!

Differentiation is a job — the storyteller's job! Drag the slider to take Sasha to ANY spot on her journey. Your job: look at the tiny slope right under her skis and tell what she feels right there, right now.

📖 You (the differentiator!) say: —
🚀 Ch.1
slope 2.0 → 20 m/s
"Wheee!"
☕ Ch.2
slope 0.5 → 5 m/s
"Phew…"
😱 Ch.3
slope 3.0 → 30 m/s
"Hold on!!"

💡 Notice: you never needed the WHOLE mountain — only the tiny slope under her skis! That "slope right here" is called the derivative. Telling it at every spot = differentiation! 🔍📖

🌀 New stories, new twists! Sasha's feelings changed in big chapters. But a bouncing ball's feelings change every blink — and sometimes it STOPS! And a rocket's slope keeps GROWING the whole flight! Same storytelling tool — wilder stories! 👇

⚽🎮 Demo 4: Bobo the Bouncing Ball!

Meet Bobo! He bounces 3 times — HIGH, medium, tiny. Drag the time slider ⏱️ and tell HIS story: zooming up? floating at the top? whizzing down? Watch the black tangent stick — that stick IS the slope right HERE, right NOW!

📖 You (the differentiator!) say: —
⏫ UP
slope > 0
"Zooming up!"
🎈 TOP
slope = 0
"Floating…"
⏬ DOWN
slope < 0
"Whizzing down!"

🤯 BIG discovery: at the very top Bobo STOPS — slope = ZERO! Slopes can be positive (up ⏫), zero (paused! 🎈), even negative (down ⏬). A good storyteller tells ALL of them! 📖

🎓 Secret vocabulary — DIFFERENTIATION = storytelling: telling Sasha's journey — "Chapter 1 she shouts 'Wheee!' (20 m/s), Chapter 2 she sips cocoa (5 m/s), Chapter 3 she screams 'Hold on!' (30 m/s)" — IS differentiation! Each chapter's feeling (the slope: 2.0, 0.5, 3.0) is called the derivative, written dy/dx or f′(x). Plus two twists: Bobo's slope can be ZERO (floating at the top! 🎈) or negative (falling ⏬!), and Rocky's slope keeps GROWING (acceleration — the second derivative! 🚀).
