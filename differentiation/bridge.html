The Big Link! 🌉

Slope and area UNDO each other — prove it and earn your Calculus Star! ⭐

🌉 Final mission! Slope chops a trip into tiny moment-speeds (Modules 1–3). Area glues tiny speeds back into a whole trip (Module 4). They UNDO each other — like zipping and unzipping a jacket! 🧥

Grown-up mathematicians call this the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus. Prove it in the demo, pass the 5-star quiz, and graduate! 🎓

🎮 Demo: Chop it, Glue it, Get it Back!

A car drives at 60 km/h for 3 hours. Chop (slope/derivative): distance line → speed line. Glue (area/integral): speed area → back to 180 km!

Total trip: 60 km/h × 3 h = 180 km 🎉

Slope chopped it apart, area glued it back. They UNDO each other — the Fundamental Theorem! 🌉

📖 The Big Link, in one sentence: adding up all the tiny speeds (area/integral) rebuilds the whole trip that chopping (slope/derivative) took apart. Symbolically: if distance = D, speed = D′, then D = ∫D′. Zip ↔ unzip! 🧥

📚 Your Calculus Dictionary

Steepness (slope) = rise ÷ run. The seed of everything! ⛰️
Derivative = slope at one instant (speedometer!). Written dy/dx. 🏎️
Power Rule = xⁿ → nxⁿ⁻¹. The 1-line magic trick! ✨
Integral = area under a curve. The ∫ sign = S for Sum. 🍕
Integration = the adding-up process (robots!). 🤖
