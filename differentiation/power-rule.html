The Power-Rule Machine!

Curvy slopes change everywhere — but one magic trick tames them all!

🛝 Straight lines have ONE slope. But a curvy slide gets steeper as you go down! What is the slope of a curve at one exact spot? 🤔

Zoom in super close 🔍 and the tiny piece looks straight — that mini-slope is the answer. For the curve y = x² the slopes follow a magic pattern: at x they equal 2x. Feed powers into the hungry machine below and discover the 1-line trick! 😋
😋

The Hungry Power Machine!

Type a power like 2, 3 or 4, press FEED, and watch the magic trick!

y = x ➡️
Machine is hungry… feed it a number! 😋
📖 The magic trick (Power Rule): bring the power number down in FRONT, then subtract 1 from the power.
y = x² → slope = 2x¹ = 2x • y = x³ → slope = 3x² • y = x⁴ → slope = 4x³.
One line of magic, and you can differentiate ANY power! 🪄
