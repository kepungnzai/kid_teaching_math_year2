Area = Integration!

Count pizza squares under a graph — the second half of calculus!

🍕 Imagine a cheesy pizza slice trapped under a sloping line. How many cheese squares fit underneath? Counting squares under a graph is called integration, and the answer is called an integral.

The fancy sign ∫ is just a stretched-out letter S for Sum — add up all the tiny squares! ➕

🎮 Demo 1: Count the Pizza Squares! 🍕

The line y = x from 0 to 4 makes a pizza triangle. Each grid square = 1 cheese square. Count them, then check the triangle shortcut!

🟧 Filled squares = ?  •  Shortcut ½ × 4 × 4 = 8

Half-squares pair up into wholes — the total is exactly 8! Area under y = x is an integral: ∫x dx = 8. ✨

📖 Read the pizza: columns under y = x have heights 0,1,2,3,4. Stack them: 0+1+2+3+4 = 10… but each square is only HALF-filled at the diagonal! Pairs make 8 wholes. Shortcut: triangle area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 4 × 4 = 8. Counting squares = integration! ➕

🤖 Demo 2: Rectangle Robots!

Under the curve y = x² from 0 to 3. Skinny robots guess the area — more robots = better guess! True area = 9.

🤖 Robots guess ?  •  Truth = 9  

Infinite robots = perfect answer. That limit idea powers all of integration! 🔬
