🗺️ X & Y: The Secret Map of Every Graph! ✨

Meet X and Y!

Every graph is like a treasure map 🗺️ — and it has two magic lines called axes (that's the plural of axis!).

The X-axis lies flat along the bottom ➡️. It tells you WHAT we are talking about — like where apples come from, or what kinds of cakes are for sale!

The Y-axis stands up tall on the side ⬆️. It tells you HOW MANY or HOW MUCH — like counting apples or reading prices!

🧠 X goes across, Y goes up! Read across to find the thing, then look up to find its number! 🔍

🔍 The Two Super Lines

➡️
X-Axis (across)

The flat line at the bottom. It shows the categories — the "what" of our story!

⬆️
Y-Axis (up)

The tall line on the left. It shows the numbers — how many or how much!

🤝
The Origin (0,0)

Where X and Y meet in the corner. All counting starts here!

📍
Finding a Point

Pick a bar, slide across to see its name, then look up (or left) to read its number!

🍰 Example 2: The Cake Shop Prices

Now let's visit a cake shop! This time the X-axis shows the types of cakes, and the Y-axis shows the price in dollars. 🤑
