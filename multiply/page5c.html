💎 Diamond Ratio Game! Look at the target ratio — for example 1 green : 2 red. Tap exactly 1 green diamond and 2 red diamonds anywhere on the board. Position doesn't matter, only the ratio!

If you match it, the diamonds disappear with a sparkle and you get a point, then a brand-new ratio appears. Keep going until the board can't make the ratio anymore… then the game is over! 🎮

📖 How to Play

  1. 1 Look at the yellow banner — it shows the target ratio, for example 1 🟢 green : 2 🔴 red.
  2. 2 Tap that exact number of each colored diamond — anywhere on the board! Where they are doesn't matter, only how many of each color.
  3. 3 Watch the counters under the banner (like Red: 1/2) — when both turn green ✓ you've done it!
  4. 4 Matched diamonds pop away with sparkles ✨ and you win +1 point. A brand-new random ratio appears right away!
  5. 5 Keep playing round after round. When there aren't enough diamonds left to make any ratio… Game Over! See how many points you can score! 🏆

💡 Tips: Tap a selected diamond again to un-select it. Diamonds in colors you don't need look faded — ignore them! If you tap too many of one color, nothing bad happens, it just wiggles.
