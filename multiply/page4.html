📈 Things GROW When We Multiply! 🚀

You've learned so much already! 🎉

You know how to multiply by 2 🍎🍎, multiply by 3 🚗🚗🚗 and multiply by 4 🐶🐶🐶🐶.

Now it's time to bring them all together and discover one amazing secret 👀:

When you multiply, numbers GROW — and bigger multipliers make them grow much faster! 🌱🚀

Watch ×2 grow, then ×3 grow, then ×4 grow — and finally we'll race them all together! 🏁

× 2 ➜ +2 every step 🍎 × 3 ➜ +3 every step 🚗 × 4 ➜ +4 every step 🐶

🍎 Multiply by 2 — Growing!

×2 grows by adding +2 every step: 2 → 4 → 6 → 8 → 10!

Each new step drops in one more group of 2 apples. 🍎🍎

🚗 Multiply by 3 — Growing!

×3 grows by adding +3 every step: 3 → 6 → 9 → 12 → 15!

Each new step drops in one more group of 3 cars. 🚗🚗🚗

🐶 Multiply by 4 — Growing!

×4 grows by adding +4 every step: 4 → 8 → 12 → 16 → 20!

Each new step drops in one more group of 4 dogs. 🐶🐶🐶🐶

🏁 The BIG Growth Race!

NOW let's watch ×2, ×3 and ×4 grow at the same time!

🐢 ×2 plods along slowly · 🐇 ×3 hops faster · 🐆 ×4 sprints away! Who will go furthest in 5 steps?

Did you see it? In every row, ×4 makes the biggest number ⭐ — and by step 5 it reaches 20, which is twice as big as ×2's 10!

🧠 What did we learn?

1 Every multiplication table grows by the same amount each step: ×2 jumps +2, ×3 jumps +3, and ×4 jumps +4.
2 A bigger multiplier (like ×4) makes numbers grow much faster than a smaller one (like ×2). 🐆
3 Multiplication is like super-charged counting — we can reach big numbers in only a few steps! 🌟
