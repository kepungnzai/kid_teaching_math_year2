⚖️ Ratios: Rectangle A vs Rectangle B 📏

What is a ratio?

A ratio compares two things. We write it with a colon, like A : B = 2 : 1, and read it as "two to one".

2 : 1 means: Rectangle A is 2 times as big as Rectangle B. In multiplication words:

A = 2 × B ✨

🎮 Try It: Change the Ratio!

Pick a ratio below and watch what happens: Rectangle B stays 1 unit tall, while Rectangle A grows to match the first number of the ratio.

That's the secret of ratios — A is always the first number × B!

🍎 Apples: Aaron vs Zana

👦 Aaron and 👧 Zana pick apples in a ratio of Aaron : Zana = 2 : 1.

That means: for every 1 apple Zana has, Aaron has 2. In multiplication words:

Aaron's apples = 2 × Zana's apples 🍎🍎 per 🍎
