➗ Division — Share the Big Numbers!

Sharing time! You already know 2 × 5 = 10. But what about 20 ÷ 2, 50 ÷ 5, 200 ÷ 4 and 77 ÷ 11? 🤯

Division just means sharing fairly. 20 ÷ 2 asks: "if we share 20 sweets between 2 friends, how many sweets does each friend get?"

And here's the magician's secret: division is multiplication backwards! To solve 20 ÷ 2, just ask 2 × ? = 20. ✨

➗

Our four big challenges

We'll solve these by flipping them into multiplications we already know — no calculator needed! 👇

20 ÷ 2 = 10 50 ÷ 5 = 10 200 ÷ 4 = 50 77 ÷ 11 = 7

💡 20 ÷ 2 means "20 sweets, 2 friends". Each friend gets 10 — because 2 × 10 = 20!

💡 77 ÷ 11 means "77 sweets, 11 friends". Each friend gets 7 — because 11 × 7 = 77!

🧠 The division recipe (only 3 steps!)

1 Read it as sharing: 50 ÷ 5 means "share 50 things into 5 equal groups."
2 Flip it: ask "5 × WHAT makes 50?" Count in 5s: 5, 10, 15 … 50. That's 10 jumps!
3 Check it: multiply back. 5 × 10 = 50 ✓ — division and multiplication are best friends!
