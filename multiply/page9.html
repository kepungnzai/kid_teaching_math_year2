✖ Old-School "Multiply & Add" — Big Numbers!

Time to go BIG! You already know small times tables like 3 × 5 = 15. But what about 20 × 11, 3 × 13 and 20 × 21? 🤯

Long, long ago — before calculators — kids did it the old-school way: MULTIPLY by each digit, then ADD the rows together! No calculator needed!

Watch the wizard on this page do it step by step, exactly the way it's written in school workbooks. ✨

🧮

Our three big challenges

We will solve these the old-school way. Notice how a 2-digit number on top gets multiplied twice (once by the ones digit, once by the tens digit) and then we add the two rows! 👇

20 × 11 = 220 3 × 13 = 39 20 × 21 = 420

💡 Did you know? 3 × 13 is exactly the same as 13 × 3 — we just write the bigger number on top so the columns look tidy!

💡 A number like 20 is 2 tens + 0 ones. Multiplying by the tens digit adds a zero at the end (2 × 20 = 40 → 400).

🧠 The old-school recipe (only 4 steps!)

1 Line up the numbers — ones under ones, tens under tens. The bigger number goes on top.
2 Multiply the big number by the ones digit of the bottom number. Write that row first.
3 Multiply the big number by the tens digit of the bottom number. Write a 0 placeholder in the ones column, then the rest. (That's the "× 10" trick!)
4 ADD the two rows together — now you've got the big answer! 🎉
